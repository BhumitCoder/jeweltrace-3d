Must have — fixes your Google errors right now
/robots.txt
Critical
Tells Google and AI bots which pages to crawl. Missing or wrong = indexing blocked. Must list your sitemap URL.
/sitemap.xml
Critical
List of all your pages with correct https://www URLs. Wrong URLs here = redirect errors in Search Console.
/sitemap-images.xml
Critical
Separate sitemap just for images. Helps Google index your product/service photos. You already have this but URLs are wrong.
SEO files — get you ranked on Google
canonical tag (in every page head)
Important
Not a file — a tag inside each page. Tells Google "this is the real URL". Prevents duplicate content penalty.
schema JSON-LD (in every page head)
Important
Structured data. Enables sitelinks, rich results, star ratings in Google. 11 types needed for your site.
/images/og-image.jpg
Important
1200×630px preview image shown when your link is shared on WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn. Should show your logo + tagline.
/favicon.ico + /apple-touch-icon.png
Important
Small icon shown in browser tab and phone home screen. Google uses presence of favicon as a quality signal.
AEO files — get found by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini
/llms.txt
AEO 2025
Brand new 2025 standard. Tells ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude what your website is about so they can answer questions about your business.
/ai.txt
AEO 2025
Gives permission to AI crawlers to use your content for answers. Without it some AI bots skip your site.
Trust & legal files — required for E-E-A-T score
/privacy-policy
Trust
Legal requirement in India. Google gives lower trust score to sites without one. Required for Google Ads too.
/terms-of-service
Trust
Builds trust with Google and visitors. Shows your site is a legitimate business.
/contact
Trust
Must have full address, phone, email, Google Maps embed. Google checks this to verify you are a real local business.
Performance files — faster site = better ranking
/.htaccess or /vercel.json
Performance
Server config. Enable gzip compression, browser caching, HTTPS redirect, www redirect — all in one file.
/manifest.json
Performance
Makes your site installable as an app on phones. Google gives ranking boost to PWA-ready sites.
/.well-known/security.txt
Trust
Tells Google your site takes security seriously. Small signal but takes 2 minutes to add.
