## What We Do
JewelsReport issues four types of gemological certification:

1. **Lab Grown Diamond Reports** — Full 4Cs grading (cut, colour, clarity, carat) with origin disclosure for CVD and HPHT lab-grown diamonds
2. **Natural Diamond / Jewellery Reports** — Complete appraisal of jewellery including metal, stone weight and setting details
3. **Gemstone Reports** — Scientific identification and treatment disclosure for sapphire, ruby, emerald and all coloured stones
4. **Lab Grown Jewellery Reports** — Full certification of finished pieces containing lab-grown stones
